How the visit and provider comparison work

A provider may ask where the moisture is showing, whether it comes and goes, and what plumbing is nearby. They can then decide what to inspect. Some situations call for a visual check, while other cases may involve additional methods chosen for the suspected source. The specific process varies, so ask the plumber to explain the plan before work begins.

As you compare providers, look for direct answers to these points:
- What will the inspection include?
- What could make the source hard to locate?
- Which findings would lead to a repair recommendation?
- Which parts of the visit or repair affect the cost?

You may hear source, meaning the point where water is escaping, and access, meaning how easily the plumbing can be reached. Diagnosis refers to identifying the cause.
