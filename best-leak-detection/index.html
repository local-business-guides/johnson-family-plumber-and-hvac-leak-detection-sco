What leak detection can tell you

A leak can show up as a visible drip, a damp spot, or a change you can’t easily explain. Best leak detection starts with identifying what’s known, such as where moisture appears and when it started, then checking likely plumbing sources. Finding the source helps you decide what repair is needed instead of guessing from the symptom alone.

What to ask and expect during an inspection

A plumber may begin by asking when the issue started, where you see water, and whether the signs change over time. Depending on the situation, a provider may inspect visible plumbing and use suitable listening or moisture-checking methods. The exact approach depends on the suspected source and access to the area, so ask what the inspection will involve in your case.

When comparing providers, consider whether they:
- Explain the inspection steps in plain language.
- Tell you what findings would lead to a repair recommendation.
- Discuss the likely work and cost factors before proceeding.
- Make clear what is included in the visit and what may be separate.

A few terms you may hear: a leak source is the point where water escapes; access means how easily a plumber can reach the suspected plumbing; and diagnosis is the process of identifying the cause of the problem. Best leak detection is about locating the likely source, not assuming every damp area has the same cause.
