Choosing a provider and understanding plumbing terms

When comparing plumbers, find out how each provider will assess the issue and explain the recommended work. Ask what is included in the proposed scope, whether additional work would be discussed with you first, and who will carry it out. You can also ask about technician qualifications and how the provider will communicate any changes to the plan. These points help you compare the service being offered rather than relying on a general description alone.

A few useful terms:
- Fixture: A connected plumbing item such as a sink, toilet, or faucet.
- Supply line: Plumbing that carries water to a fixture or part of a building.
- Shutoff valve: A valve used to stop water flow to a fixture or section of plumbing.
